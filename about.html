Philosophy

Unified Martial Arts

Tong-Il Moo-Do is a comprehensive, unified martial art discipline originating from South Korea. The martial art was founded in 1979 by Grandmaster Dr. Joon Ho Seuk and is globally overseen by the World Tong-Il Moo-Do Federation.

Unlike systems that focus exclusively on combat, Tong-Il Moo-Do synthesizes circular and linear movements from various martial arts styles (including Taekwondo, Judo, Karate, and Aikido). It emphasizes the harmonization of mind and body, self-control, and the development of moral character.

The Zambia Tong-Il Moo-Do Federation (ZMTIMDF) was established to bring these values to Southern Africa. By providing professional instruction, we empower the youth of Zambia with sportsmanship, discipline, and essential self-defense skills.
