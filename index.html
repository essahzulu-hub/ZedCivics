What We Do

Building Strong Bodies and Stronger Communities

From school programs to national gradings, our federation supports martial arts training that strengthens confidence, respect, and teamwork across Zambia.

Training Excellence

Our curriculum blends traditional Tong-Il Moo-Do techniques with modern training methods to develop every student’s physical and mental capacity.

Community Impact

We partner with schools, youth centers, and community groups to promote self-discipline, character development, and safe self-defense.

National Programs

The federation hosts grading exams, referee training, and championship events that keep Zambia connected to the global Tong-Il Moo-Do family.
